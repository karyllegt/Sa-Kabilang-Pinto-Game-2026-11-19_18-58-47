## Art and audio
Original v1 sprites and audio exported from the project's own browser prototype (`reference/assets/`). New assets are made in the same style for this project.

The presentation upgrade recovers detailed artwork from the earlier Codex project and adds AI-assisted Bellkeeper, Lola, map, terrain and hazard atlases. Generation prompts are recorded with the assets; do not attribute that work as hand-drawn art by Mary or Ronn.
