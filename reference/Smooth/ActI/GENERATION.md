# Act I smooth preview art sources

The three source images in this folder were created with the built-in ImageGen tool using the [Act I concept](../act-i-storybook-concept.png) as a style reference. They are AI-assisted illustrations. The preview builder crops the sheets and fits each image to the corresponding Unity Sprite rectangle and pivot.

| Source file | Preview use |
| --- | --- |
| `tala-poses.png` | Girl Tala in idle, run, rise, apex and fall. Preview frames use the original timing and loop settings. |
| `props-sheet.png` | Wood ledge, checkpoint parol, red spike strip and full vitality heart. |
| `capiz-background.png` | The Act I capiz and wood backdrop. |

## Prompts

### Tala

Create a clean transparent game sprite animation sheet for Tala in Sa Kabilang Pinto. The reference is the Act I storybook concept and defines her look, not the layout. Smooth high-resolution hand-drawn 2D cel illustration. The same young Filipino girl in every pose: round face, black ponytail, cream camisa blouse, red sash and red patadyong skirt, bare feet, a held small golden five-point Filipino parol. Face RIGHT in all poses, side view. Dark violet outlines, soft cel shading, minimal gold glow tightly inside lantern silhouette, no light wash outside sprite, readable at a small size. Eight full-body sprites arranged in a PRECISE 4-column by 2-row equal grid across the canvas, centered inside cells, at consistent body height and foot level with wide transparent margin between cells. Top row, left to right: neutral idle with planted feet; idle breath alternate with same planted feet; run contact right leg forward; run passing right leg beneath body. Bottom row: run contact left leg forward; run passing left leg beneath body; jump rising with knees bent; falling legs extended downward. Distinct silhouettes but consistent head size, clothing, colors and handheld lantern shape. Keep the full figure and the parol inside its cell. Absolutely no labels, text, sheet borders, baseline, scenery, drop shadows, cell fill, checkerboard effect, or pixel art. Use actual alpha throughout the empty space.

### Props

Create a transparent asset sheet for Sa Kabilang Pinto. Image reference is a style and motif guide. Smooth hand-drawn Philippine folklore storybook game illustrations, soft cel shading and clean antialiased contours at high resolution. EXACTLY four distinct isolated props arranged in a PRECISE equal 2x2 grid, wide transparent gutters, no touching edges. Top left: straight horizontal wooden platform with a perfectly flat and clear top edge and completely filled wooden face, a simple carved narra trim. Broad 3:1 tile strip, side-on, uniform wood at left and right for repetition, no protrusions above the top and no glow. Top right: five-point Filipino gold parol with short narrow festive tassels, warm cream center and thin bronze frame, symmetrical, front-on, NO pole or stand. Bottom left: six short red-tipped black iron spikes in one side-on line, with a muted deep red horizontal base. Broad about 3:1, spikes extend to the top edge of their little bounding box, completely transparent between tips. Bottom right: a single warm red enamel heart health icon with tiny pale highlight and tidy bronze edge, bold shape, front-on. Actual transparency in every empty area; no scenery, labels, baseline, cast shadows, title, grid lines, or pixel art.

### Backdrop

Create one widescreen 16:9 game background only, a straight side-on view across the interior wall of a Filipino ancestral house-tower for Sa Kabilang Pinto. Use reference only for art direction. Smooth high resolution 2D storybook illustration with restrained texture, not pixel art. Repeating architectural rhythm of wooden panels, dark muted brown carved narra beams, large rectangular capiz-shell windows, dark violet night and soft silhouettes of tropical leaves visible beyond. Keep the wall quite subdued, with restrained warm light in some window squares, medium-dark violet-brown wood, low local contrast and no distracting bright points. Clean straight perspective as for a side-scrolling platform game. Design left and right edges to join quietly if repeated. Full bleed. The image is a flat backdrop: absolutely no foreground platforms or floors, no character, door, parol objects, hazards, lettering, interface, frame or vignette.
