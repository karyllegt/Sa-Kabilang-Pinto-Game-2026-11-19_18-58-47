# Smooth environments art provenance

The seven source PNGs below were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this stage. They are AI-assisted illustrations. The Act I background used the approved smooth Capiz prototype as a style reference. The source files here retain the ImageGen output. The Unity textures are the cropped and fitted derivatives under `Assets/Art/Smooth/Environments`. The preview reuses the earlier smooth wooden ledge in Act I. The neutral flood-body Sprite is a generated alpha gradient used by the original flood renderer.

The runtime terrain sprites use the original world size, pivots, and tile border dimensions. The Spirit and Inverted scenery layers are cosmetic and are tinted in the isolated preview at runtime.
