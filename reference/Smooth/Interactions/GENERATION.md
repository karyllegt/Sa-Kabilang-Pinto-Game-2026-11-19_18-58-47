# Smooth interactions art provenance

Six transparent source atlases were generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. The illustrations are AI-assisted. This folder records the source imagery and prompts for the Unity-ready art in `Assets/Art/Smooth/Interactions`.

The source atlas cells were cropped at their alpha boundaries and fitted to the source sprite pivots, world sizes, and tile borders. The locked exit is a dim derivative of the same door used for the open state. The warning line is a generated white alpha mask rendered through the existing red warning color. The original state arrays and warning cadence drive these sprites. Existing particle tints are applied to neutral atlas derivatives.

The flood body and ripple strip were drawn in the [environment pass](../Environments/GENERATION.md) and are reused through the preview catalogs. Dash and dimension flares use the atlas sprites and read the existing dash timer and door event. Their scale and fade are presentation only.

The campaign rollout replaced the regular and gauntlet spike derivatives with red-heavy silhouettes for phone readability. See the [rollout source record](../Rollout/GENERATION.md) for the replacement prompt. This page retains the original atlas source and prompts.

Upper left: Filipino carved wooden door with brass capiz lattice trim and vividly teal smoke filling doorway (Spirit); upper right: same carved door with glowing violet portal and downward curling motifs (Inverted). Lower left: matching doorway with warm creamy gold light pouring only within the lintel and threshold (safe return home); lower right: statelier twin brass doors with central gold parol keyhole and warm gold rim, for final exit. Rich dark Philippine wood and contrasting bright interior lights as clear code, avoid large exterior bloom. Portrait objects with at least 12% clear padding.
