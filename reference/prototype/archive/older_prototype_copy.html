HOW TO PLAY

Climb each haunted tower to the lantern above. Every room has a fork: take the ledges of the waking world, or enter a doorway.

TEAL doors open the Spirit world: new ledges appear, but your shadow follows your path 4 seconds behind. Any door is refuge.

VIOLET doors flip gravity upward in the Inverted world: walk off a ledge and rise to the next. Rise to HOME above.

Red means danger. Gold means safety. Teal marks spirit paths.

DASH spends flame and makes you untouchable for a moment. WARD stuns or harms creatures and parries the Bellkeeper's toll. Light lanterns to save.

LETTERS hidden in each tower are more than memos. Every one you find teaches Tala a new skill: double jump, the Lambong veil, the Liwanag bolt, and more.

Keys: A/D or arrows move. Space jump (again in air with Double Jump). Q dash. F ward. C veil. X bolt. E door. Esc pause. Gamepad supported.
