### Naming convention (proposed)
- Keep the provided file names as they are (they are referenced by the importer script and docs): lowercase snake_case with a type prefix.
- Sprites PNG: `spr_<thing>[_<variant>]_<frame>.png` for characters/items (`spr_pl_girl_2.png`), `tile_<kind>_<w>x<h>.png`, `hazard_<kind>[_<state>]_<w>x<h>.png`, `door_<kind>_<w>x<h>.png`, `ui_<name>.png`, `icon_skill_<id>.png` (new, e.g. `icon_skill_veil.png`), `fx_<name>[_<frame>].png`, `bg_<name>.png`, `portrait_<who>.png`. Pixel size goes in the name when the sprite is not a character frame.
- Audio OGG: `sfx_<event>.ogg` (new ones: `sfx_double_jump`, `sfx_veil_on`, `sfx_veil_off`, `sfx_bolt_fire`, `sfx_bolt_hit`, `sfx_burn`, `sfx_skill_learned`, `sfx_letter`, `sfx_toll_hit`), `mus_<context>.ogg` (`mus_menu`, `mus_a1..a3`, `mus_boss`).
- Code: PascalCase types, one type per file, namespaces `Skp.<Folder>` (e.g. `Skp.Save.SaveService`). Assets: ScriptableObjects `<Type>_<Name>.asset` (`LevelConfig_01_FiestaNight.asset`, `Skill_veil.asset`).
- `DECISIONS.md`: repo root (this file). `Tests/MANUAL_QA.md` and `Tests/Screenshots/`: top-level `Tests/` folder beside `Assets/` (outside Assets so screenshots are not imported). `CREDITS.md`: repo root.

## 2026-11-20 · M2 Player feel

| # | Topic | Decision |

| M2-4 | Jump height | Measured from the prototype's own step: full-hold apex **62.5 px = 3.906 tiles** on tick 16, landing on tick 34; a 1-tick tap reaches 19.3 px; a 6-tick hold 42.025 px. ## 2026-11-21 · M4 Level framework and levels 1 to 3

| # | Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M4-1 | Approach | **Ported the prototype's seeded generator** (`buildLevel` and `route`), not hand-authored tilemaps. `LevelGenerator` runs statement by statement in the prototype's own coordinates (px, y down) with the same xorshift32 RNG (`SeededRandom`), the same three streams (R, R2, RS) and the same short-circuit draw order. It is checked against 30 layouts dumped from the real prototype (`tests/prototype/dump_levels.js` -> `tests/prototype/fixtures/prototype_levels.json`: 9 levels on Standard with no skills and with all skills, levels 1 to 3 on Story and Nightmare); every ledge, hazard, door, item, creature and lantern must match. `LevelWorldFactory` converts to Unity units (y up, ground floor top = 0). |
| M4-2 | Exact numbers | Level rates are `double` and float difficulty multipliers are rounded back to their decimals (`LevelSession.Exact`, 0.6f -> 0.6) so comparisons against random draws match JavaScript doubles exactly. |
| M4-3 | Creature direction | The prototype picks an Anino's or tikbalang's starting direction with `Math.random()` (not seeded). The port alternates it by index, which is deterministic; this is the only intended difference from the fixture and is excluded from the comparison. |
| M4-4 | Data | `LevelConfig` assets (`Assets/Data/Levels/LevelConfig_01..09`) wrap a `LevelSpec` (the prototype's LEVELS row: rooms, act, inverted route, enemy types, spike/root/crumble rates, flood, boss, intro, clue, first hint) plus the skill asset. `SkillDefinition` assets (`Assets/Data/Skills/Skill_*.asset`) hold the prototype's eight skill texts. `LevelArt` holds the sprite references. The setup creates them once and never overwrites Inspector edits. All nine levels are generated; levels 4 to 9 still need M7 features (roots and flood visuals, manananggal flight, boss). |
| M4-5 | Mechanics | Crumbling ledges: 42 ticks after landing they vanish for 220. Moving ledges swing `base + sin(t * speed + phase) * amplitude` and carry Tala. Spikes always hurt; roots strike in the last 40 of 150 ticks; thorn walls hurt until burnt. Pickups: flame +15 (score 10), fragment max flame +5 up to 160 and full flame (200), letter (150, all copies taken). Anino and tikbalang patrol their ledge, turn at the ends, hurt on contact through `CanBeHurt`; the ward kills an Anino and stuns others (170 ticks, -1 hp). Lantern +100 score.
